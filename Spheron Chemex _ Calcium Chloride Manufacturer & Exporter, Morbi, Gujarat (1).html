Frequently asked questions


What is the difference between flakes and prills?

Both are about 74–80% dihydrate. Flakes are thin and irregular, prills are round beads with less dust and better flow.

Which grade is used for oil drilling?

Usually 94–98% anhydrous or 77–80% flakes, depending on the brine density you need.

What is the HSN code?

Calcium Chloride falls under HSN 28272000.

Do you give samples and documents?

Yes. Ask for a sample, TDS, MSDS and certificate of analysis in the enquiry form.

How should Calcium Chloride be stored?

It absorbs moisture from air. Keep it in sealed bags in a cool, dry place, off the floor.

Is it suitable for food use?

Only food grade material is. Tell us if you need food or pharma grade and we will confirm availability and documents.

Do you export?

Yes. Tell us the destination port and quantity and we will quote accordingly.
